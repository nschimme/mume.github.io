# About MUME

**MUME** (Multi-Users in Middle-earth) is a free, text-based MMORPG (MUD) set in J.R.R. Tolkien's world of Middle-earth. Operating continuously since 1992, MUME allows players to explore a vast, highly detailed recreation of Middle-earth during the late Third Age.
