# MUME Chronicles

The development and world history of MUME is divided into major historical chronicles:

## M7 Chronicles
The initial golden age of expansion, covering the establishment of the West-gate of Moria, Rivendell, and early War of the Ring mechanics.

## M8 Chronicles
Major system overhauls including rewritten spell systems, enhanced combat actions, and expanded evil race gameplay.

## M9 Chronicles
Modern refinements, web interface integration, client protocol additions, and ongoing community preservation.
