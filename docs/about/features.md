# MUME Features

Multi-Users in Middle-earth provides a rich and complex environment built around J.R.R. Tolkien's legendarium:

- **Faithful Middle-earth Geography**: Explore thousands of uniquely described rooms, from the Shire and Rivendell to Moria and Mirkwood.
- **Dynamic World Mechanics**: Real-time day/night cycles, seasonal weather changes, terrain movement penalties, and dynamic river currents.
- **Races & Classes**: Play as Elves, Hobbits, Dwarves, Men of the West, or join Sauron's forces as Orcs, Trolls, and Black Numenoreans.
- **Strategic Combat & Magic**: Weapon specialization, spell casting, rideable mounts, and tactical positioning.
- **Herbalism & Crafting**: Gather herbs to brew potions, forge equipment, and customize your character.
