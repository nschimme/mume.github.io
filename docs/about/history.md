# History of MUME

MUME was created in 1992 by Pier Donini (Eru) at EPFL / HEIG-VD in Switzerland. Built initially on DikuMUD base code, MUME rapidly evolved with custom mechanics, original maps, and extensive Middle-earth fidelity.

Over thirty years, generations of players, developers, and Valar have contributed to building one of the longest-running online multiplayer worlds in existence.
