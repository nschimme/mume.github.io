# Stopping for the Day & Renting

DO NOT type `quit` or simply close your browser window while carrying gear! Your equipment will fall to the ground and be lost.

To keep your items, go to an inn and `rent`. Your gear is stored safely until you log in again. `offer` tells you the daily rent cost at the inn.
