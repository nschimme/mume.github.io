# 📄 Student Quickstart Cheatsheet

Welcome to **MUME (Multi-Users in Middle-earth)**! You are about to enter a rich, text-based interactive world set in Tolkien's Middle-earth. Use this quick reference guide to connect, navigate, and record your lab answers.

---

## 🚀 1. How to Launch the Web Client

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, Safari).
2. Click the link or type the URL:
   <div style="text-align: center; margin: 1.5rem 0;">
     <a href="https://mume.org/play" target="_self" rel="external" class="vp-button brand" style="font-size: 1.2rem; padding: 0.8rem 2rem;">Launch MUME Web Client 🚀</a>
   </div>
3. When the screen loads, choose **Guest Login** or enter your assigned character name.

---

## 🧭 2. Basic Movement & Navigation

Movement in MUME uses compass directions. You can type the full word or just the single-letter abbreviation:

| Command | Abbreviation | Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `north` | `n` | Walk North |
| `south` | `s` | Walk South |
| `east` | `e` | Walk East |
| `west` | `w` | Walk West |
| `up` | `u` | Climb or walk Upward |
| `down` | `d` | Climb or walk Downward |
| `exits` | `ex` | Display all visible paths out of your current room |

---

## 🔍 3. Essential Inspection Commands

| Command | Usage | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `look` | `l` | Re-read the full description of your current room |
| `examine <item>` | `ex sword` | Inspect a specific object in detail |
| `inventory` | `i` or `inv` | List all items currently carried in your hands/backpack |
| `equipment` | `eq` | Show items currently worn on your body |
| `score` | `sc` | Display your character's stats, level, and health status |

---

## 📝 4. How to Copy Output for Lab Submissions

When completing lab assignments:

1. Use your mouse cursor to highlight the relevant text in the MUME terminal window.
2. Press `Ctrl + C` (Windows/Chromebook) or `Cmd + C` (Mac) to copy the highlighted text.
3. Switch to your assignment Google Doc, Canvas entry, or submission form and press `Ctrl + V` or `Cmd + V` to paste your output.

::: tip Submission Pro Tip
Keep your MUME terminal window open alongside your lab worksheet using side-by-side split screen (`Alt + [` and `Alt + ]` on Chromebooks).
:::
