# 📚 Humanities & Literature Curriculum Track

Welcome to the **MUME Humanities & Literature Curriculum Track**. Text-based MUDs offer a unique interdisciplinary bridge between classic literature, spatial narrative theory, game design, and digital archaeology.

As a multi-decade adaptation of J.R.R. Tolkien's legendarium, **Multi-Users in Middle-earth (MUME)** provides high school and university literature classes with an active-learning laboratory to explore narrative worldbuilding, textual topography, and digital preservation.

---

## 📅 Curriculum Options: 45-Min Labs vs. 1-Week Unit Block

Educators can deliver these modules as standalone 45-minute lab sessions or combine them into a 5-day unit block:

```
           [ North: Westron Road ]
                      ▲
                      │
[ West: Tavern ] <── [ Beer Garden ] ──> [ East: Market Square ]
                      │
                      ▼
           [ South: Village Gates ]
```

```
[Day 1: Spatial Narrative] ➔ [Day 2: Room Topography] ➔ [Day 3: Game History] ➔ [Day 4: Community Lore] ➔ [Day 5: Capstone Room Design]
```

### Option A: Express 45-Minute Single Period Labs
* **Lab 1 (45 min):** [Spatial Narrative & Tolkien Worldbuilding in Text](/community/educators/literature/lab-1-worldbuilding)
* **Lab 2 (45 min):** [MUD History & Digital Archaeology of Online Worlds](/community/educators/literature/lab-2-game-history)

### Option B: 1-Week Integrated Unit Blueprint

| Day | Topic & Activity | Student Output |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day 1** | **Spatial Narrative in Arda:** Analyze how literary geography in *The Lord of the Rings* translates into a 3D compass grid. | Comparative map analysis worksheet. |
| **Day 2** | **Textual Topography & Sensory Writing:** Examine MUME room descriptions for atmospheric details, dynamic weather, and spatial prose. | Room description critique paper. |
| **Day 3** | **Digital Archaeology & MUD History:** Investigate MUME (running continuously since 1992) as an early precursor to modern MMORPGs. | Historical timeline essay. |
| **Day 4** | **Community Artifacts & Player Chronicles:** Explore player-authored tales, board posts, and guild histories. | Primary source analysis report. |
| **Day 5** | **Capstone Narrative Design Project:** Draft a canonical Middle-earth room description complete with exits, secrets, and sensory details. | Submitted room description & peer review. |
